Getting Started

👥 Create a Book Club

Click Create Group on the dashboard. Give your club a name, description, genre, and reading pace. You can also set your current book right away. Once created, you'll be taken to your club page where you can invite friends.

🚪 Join an Existing Club

Click Join Club to browse all clubs. Search by name to find one you like, then send a join request. The club creator will get an email and can approve or decline your request. You'll be notified by email when they respond.

🔍 Search for Books

Click Search Books to explore titles using the Open Library database. If you've set reading preferences in your profile, we'll show personalized suggestions automatically. Search by title, author, or keyword to find your next great read.
